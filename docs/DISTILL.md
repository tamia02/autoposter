# Distill: Personal Branding LinkedIn Post Style

This file captures the typical writing, structure, and edits used by personal-branding experts like Jasmine, Alic, Lara, Amosta, and similar creators.

## Voice and tone

- Personal and honest: start from a real moment or decision.
- Confident but not arrogant: share what you learned, not just what you achieved.
- Easy-to-scan: use short paragraphs, bullets, and spacing.
- Relatable details: include a small daily habit, a business insight, or a human truth.
- Action-focused: the post should leave the reader with a clear takeaway or behavior.

## Structure

1. **Hook**
   - Start with a strong first line that sets a tension or unexpected choice.
   - Example: "I quit the thing everyone told me was safe."

2. **Context**
   - Briefly explain the background: what you did, where you were, or what the expectation was.
   - Keep it concrete and specific.

3. **Insight**
   - Reveal the shift: what you learned or what changed your mind.
   - Use contrasting language: before vs after, task vs system, busy vs effective.

4. **Examples**
   - Add one or two quick examples that show how the insight applies.
   - Prefer small business or founder context.

5. **Lesson**
   - State the practical rule or framework in a simple way.
   - Example: "If you can write it down, you can automate it."

6. **CTA / close**
   - End with a clear invite: comment, DM, save for later, or try something.
   - Keep it low-pressure.

## Copywriting patterns

- Use active verbs: quit, build, automate, simplify.
- Use numbers: "3 signals", "10x better", "daily rhythm".
- Use sentence fragments for emphasis.
- Use line breaks to control pacing.
- Keep the brand voice consistent with founder/freelancer ambition.

## Poster styling cues

- No long blocks of dense text.
- Use bold formatting sparingly for the most important line.
- Keep the target reader in mind: founders, operators, early-stage teams.
- Use a pinch of urgency or curiosity: "Most people miss this." 
- Add a subtle promise: "Here’s how I did it."
