# Content Style Guide — Based on Competitor & Creator Research

## WRITING FRAMEWORK (Inspired by Jasmin Alic, Matt Barker, Lara Acosta, Charles Miller)

### Hook Rules
- First line = EVERYTHING. It must stop the scroll.
- One-line hook only. Then a line break (blank line).
- Use curiosity gaps, contrarian takes, or specific numbers
- Hook formulas that work:
  - "I [did something unexpected] — here's what happened."
  - "Most people think [common belief]. They're wrong."
  - "I spent [X time] doing [Y]. Here's what I learned."
  - "[Number] [thing] that [dream outcome] without [obstacle]"
  - "Stop doing [bad thing]. Start doing [better thing]."
  - Personal story opener: "3 years ago I was [relatable struggle]..."

### Post Structure (Matt Barker / Jasmin Alic style)
1. HOOK (1 line — curiosity, contrarian, or story)
2. [blank line]
3. CONTEXT (2-3 short lines — set up the problem)
4. [blank line]
5. VALUE (numbered list or arrow bullets → with actionable steps)
6. [blank line]
7. TAKEAWAY (1-2 lines — the "so what")
8. [blank line]
9. CTA (question or engagement prompt)

### Formatting Rules
- Short paragraphs (1-3 sentences MAX)
- Blank line between every section
- Use → or ↳ arrows for lists instead of regular bullets
- Use numbers for step-by-step frameworks
- Bold key phrases sparingly
- Keep posts 150-300 words (LinkedIn sweet spot for educational)
- Carousels: 7-15 slides, each slide = 1 idea, big readable text

### CTA Style (Jasmin Alic)
- NOT "Like and share" — that's dead
- USE conversational CTAs:
  - "Which one resonates most? Drop a number."
  - "What would you add to this list?"
  - "DM me [keyword] and I'll send you the full playbook"
  - "Save this for later. You'll need it."
  - "Repost if someone in your network needs this ♻️"

### Voice (Lara Acosta / Charles Miller)
- Write like you THINK, not like you talk
- Use "I" and "you" — never "one should" or corporate language
- Share real experiences: "Here's what I did..." not "Here's what you should do..."
- "How I" > "How to" (Charles Miller principle)
- Be opinionated. Take a stance. Lukewarm posts die.

## COMPETITOR TOPICS & CONTENT PILLARS (Based on Manthan, Divyanshi, Suprava, Axelle, Lior)

### What They Post About
1. **Lead generation frameworks** — LinkedIn funnels, cold email sequences, warm outreach
2. **AI tools for business** — ChatGPT agents, automation workflows, prompt stacks
3. **SaaS growth tactics** — B2B selling, pipeline building, demo booking
4. **Cold email strategies** — 21 hacks, reply rate optimization, personalization
5. **LinkedIn growth** — profile optimization, content strategy, engagement hacks
6. **Personal brand building** — founder stories, credibility posts, milestone posts
7. **Tool stacks** — PhantomBuster, Kleo, Smartlead, Persana AI, Zapmail
8. **Contrarian takes** — "The old way of prospecting is dead", "Most businesses aren't missing an AI hack"
9. **Step-by-step tutorials** — "How to get leads using ChatGPT agent in 25 min"
10. **Social proof stories** — client results, revenue numbers, follower milestones

### Content Mix (Lara Acosta 4-3-2-1 Framework adapted)
- 4 posts per week minimum
- 3 pillars: EDUCATE (tips/frameworks) + STORY (personal experience) + SELL (soft pitch)
- 2 formats: Text posts + Carousels (alternate)
- 1 lead magnet per week: "DM me [word] for the full guide"

### Post Types That Go Viral for Competitors
1. **Numbered list posts** — "7 lead gen hacks that actually work in 2026"
2. **Step-by-step framework** — "LinkedIn funnel to get warm leads: Step 1..."
3. **Personal journey post** — "I did everything in marketing only to learn..."
4. **Contrarian hot take** — "Stop cold DMing. Here's what to do instead."
5. **Tool/hack reveal** — "I found a way to get leads using ChatGPT agents"
6. **Before/after** — "6 months ago I had 0 clients. Today I have 100+."
7. **Comment-bait carousel** — "Comment [WORD] and I'll send you the PDF"
